# Glossary patch — single entry correction

Replace the `## Double-stack pier` entry in `public/glossary.md` with:

---

## Double-stack pier

A concrete block pier constructed with two blocks side by side in an interlocking
pattern. Required by 24 CFR 3285.303 when block pier height exceeds 36 inches.
Under HUD installation code, piers over 67 inches require engineering from a
registered professional engineer per 24 CFR 3285.303(d)(1)(iv); the FHA PFGMH
Appendix C provides a prescriptive alternative for grouted and reinforced
double-stack piers up to 80 inches. Piers over 80 inches require site-specific
engineering under both HUD and FHA rules.

---

Rationale: the previous wording ("up to 80 inches maximum") implied a blanket
prescriptive allowance to 80 in. and omitted the 67-in. HUD engineering trigger,
contradicting `foundation-systems-guide.md` and `inspector-verification-guide.md`,
which both carry the corrected 36/67/80 language.
